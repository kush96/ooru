The Department of Animal Husbandry   Veterinary Services started functioning independently from 1945. The department is playing a vital role in providing healthcare services to livestock, extension, and training to farmers, implementation of Socioeconomic programs, conducting Sample Survey and Animal Census programs periodically in the State.
The department is undertaking various developmental activities like:
Animal health and veterinary services
Cattle development
National Project for Cattle and Buffalo Breeding (NPCBB)
Poultry development
Sheep and wool development
Dairy development
Piggery development
Fodder development
Rabbit rearing development
Extension and training programmes
Implementation of socio-economic programmes, etc.
