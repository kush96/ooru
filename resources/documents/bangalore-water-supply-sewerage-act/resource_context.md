# Bangalore Water Supply and Sewerage Act, 1964

**Document:** Bangalore Water Supply and Sewerage Act, 1964. The law started in 1964; this copy includes later changes.
`txt_source.txt` contains the full Act, covering water supply as well as sewage. The pointers below focus on our first sewage questions.

**BWSSB** means Bangalore Water Supply and Sewerage Board: Bengaluru's public water and sewage agency. The Act calls it "the Board". **Sewage** is wastewater; **sewerage** is the system of pipes and facilities that carries and handles it.

**Helps answer:** "Who should maintain this sewer?" and "What is the property owner's responsibility?"

## Useful sections

- **15** (PDF p. 16): BWSSB's overall duties.
- **63-64** (pp. 31-32): which sewers belong to BWSSB and its maintenance duties.
- **66-73**: private-property drainage, connections and keeping sewage separate from rainwater.

This is a legal starting point, not proof of who owns a particular pipe. It does not provide current complaint contacts or local office coverage.
