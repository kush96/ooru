# Bengaluru district health department

**Source:** [Official district health page](https://bengaluruurban.nic.in/en/healthdept/).

**Helps with:** Understanding the district health department and finding the public services, facilities and contacts it actually lists. District administration is not the same organisation as a city corporation.

**Limit:** This is not a complete list of neighbourhood clinics or municipal health officers. Do not use it alone to assign mosquito control, sanitation or a specific street complaint to an officer. Contacts and programme details need current confirmation; page availability is not proof they are up to date. It is not medical advice or a live emergency service.

`txt_source.txt` keeps the page content and source link. From `ooru_be`:
```bash
uv run --group resources resources/documents/bengaluru-district-health-department/fetch.py
```
